# Research and Verification Charter

1. **Conservation laws are non-negotiable.** No efficiency, power, or energy claim can bypass mass/energy accounting.
2. **Falsification first.** Each hypothesis must identify what result would disprove it.
3. **Competing baselines.** Compare to credible conventional architectures under consistent duty cycle, fuel, emissions, cost, and reliability assumptions.
4. **Evidence lineage.** Retain sources, model version, inputs, units, uncertainty, and result artifacts.
5. **Independent verification.** Separate proposing/building from checking whenever practical.
6. **Negative results matter.** Preserve failed hypotheses to prevent repeated dead ends.
7. **Simulation is not hardware validation.** Clearly distinguish analytical prediction, simulated performance, bench measurement, and field demonstration.
8. **No invented breakthroughs.** Never promote an unverified effect into an engineering or commercial claim.
9. **Human control.** Hardware, safety-critical, spending, and external commitments require explicit authorization.

## Evidence labels
IDEA → ANALYTICALLY SCREENED → SIMULATED → INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED → BENCH VALIDATED → FIELD VALIDATED.

Labels describe evidence maturity, not product readiness.
