# Genesis — Founding Vision

**Established October 2026.**

## Why Genesis exists
Engineering discovery is limited by the cost of proposing, evaluating, and rejecting ideas. Genesis aims to compress this cycle by coordinating research, simulation, adversarial review, and engineering implementation into a traceable invention process.

## North star
An autonomous invention laboratory that can propose unexpected, physically plausible inventions; design decisive experiments; learn from failures; and advance validated concepts toward prototypes and useful technology.

## Operating model
1. Define a real-world performance problem and constraints.
2. Generate multiple competing physical hypotheses.
3. Challenge each hypothesis with first-principles analysis and known prior art.
4. Design falsifiable simulations and experiments.
5. Execute with pinned inputs, reproducible methods, and independent verification.
6. Preserve negative findings and uncertainty as first-class evidence.
7. Advance only independently supported claims toward hardware and commercialization.

## Relationship to existing systems
- **Argus:** scientific research and hypothesis investigation.
- **Polytrope:** physical modeling and simulation where applicable.
- **Orloom:** bounded engineering implementation and verification once operationally ready.
- **Athena:** probabilistic evaluation, evidence calibration, and decision support where useful.

These are intended roles, not claims that integrations are operational.

## Boundaries
Genesis owns invention strategy, mission records, evidence standards, experiment lineage, and decisions. It does not duplicate a coding harness, generic research engine, dashboard, or simulation runtime.

## Success
Success is not the number of ideas or simulated experiments. It is reproducible scientific insight, independently validated improvements against credible baselines, and ultimately commercially useful physical technology.

The owner retains authority over mission priorities, resource commitments, safety, and commercialization decisions.
